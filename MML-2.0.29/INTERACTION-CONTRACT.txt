MML interaction contract
========================

These rules are stable unless a release explicitly changes them.

HOME

- Tap a log row: open that row's full-screen detail view. A Home-row tap never directly launches an app
  or executes a notification action.
- Checkered-border log row: that single entry has a contextual detail action. The background
  remains dark. The border is not a pin, favorite, selection, or storage state.
- Tap the reverse-video date: append another Bloodguilt curse warning to the journal.
- Long-press the date or ordinary Home surfaces: no Settings shortcut.
- Make a log entry: open the searchable app picker.

APP PICKER

- App picker is a full-screen MML surface.
- Find an app is at the top.
- Settings is one full-width button at the bottom and is the sole direct MML Settings entry point.
- Tap one app: save one USER/app_launch entry, wait for journal acknowledgement, return MML state
  to Home, then launch that app.
- Long-press one app: open full-screen App info; do not launch it.
- List view shows one-line names. Brick view shows compact first-seven-code-point labels in a
  multi-column grid. The List/Brick preference exists in Settings, not in the picker.
- Android Home: return Home and hide the keyboard.
- Android Back: return Home without launching.

APP INFO

- App info is full-screen.
- Show app label, package name, version name/code, and a link to Android's package App info page.
- Android Back returns to the app picker.

ENTRY DETAIL

- Entry detail is full-screen.

- Long-press text: use Android's native text selection/copy behavior.
- A tap while text is selected cannot activate the entry.
- Tap app-launch detail text or blank detail space: launch the recorded package.
- Tap Bloodguilt warning detail text or blank detail space: show "Goes to internet. Continue?".
  Continue opens bloodguiltcurse.net; Cancel stays in MML. The browser is never opened before confirmation.
- Tap eligible notification detail text or blank detail space: resolve and invoke its current
  live Android content action.
- Notify exists only for one real saved entry. After success it becomes Unnotify.
- Unnotify clears durable notified state and removes that Android card without deleting the row.
- Grouped display rows have no synthetic Notify.
- Delete removes journal rows. Android log-entry notifications are then reconciled from durable
  state so unrelated cards remain correct.

ANDROID NOTIFICATION CARD

- Main tap: perform the notification's useful action. For app-launch entries this launches the app.
- Log: open the exact source MML log entry.
- Remove: unnotify that source row without opening MML.
- Cards are ongoing/non-auto-canceling and survive action use.
- A fresh MML process rebuilds cards from durable notified journal rows.
- At most one app-launch card is durable per package; notifying a newer launch row for the same
  package transfers ownership to the newer row.

NOTIFIED LOGS

- Notified logs is a full-screen MML surface.
- Settings > Logs > Notified logs is the central manager.
- Tap a listed entry: open its source log.
- Remove: unnotify only that entry.
- Clear all: clear all MML log-entry notifications without deleting journal rows.

PROVENANCE

- Entry detail metadata ends with a small Provenance line naming the capture path.
- Provenance is informational and does not open another Settings surface.

SELF NOTIFICATIONS

- MML-owned log-entry notifications are controls/copies, not journal events.
- The explicit capture-test notification is the only MML-owned notification admitted to capture.
