MML 2.0.27 changes
Prepared: 2026-11-11

SETTINGS ACCESS

- Settings now has one deliberate entry point: the full-width Settings button at the
  bottom of the app picker.
- Find an app remains alone at the top of the picker.
- Removed Settings from date long-press, Make a log entry long-press, and the Android
  notification area.
- On upgrade MML cancels the obsolete persistent Settings notification and removes its
  old channel.

DATE / BLOODGUILT WARNING

- A normal tap on the reverse-video date writes another Bloodguilt curse warning into
  the Home journal.
- The Bloodguilt warning detail action still opens https://www.bloodguiltcurse.net/.

NOTIFICATION SELF-MANAGEMENT

- Every MML log-entry notification has two actions:
  Log    - opens the exact source log entry in MML.
  Remove - clears that entry's durable Notify state and removes the Android card without
           opening MML.
- The notification's main tap keeps its original purpose. An app-launch notification
  still launches the recorded app and remains present.
- Settings > Logs now has Notified logs. This shows the current notified entries, lets a
  user open the source row or Remove one entry, and provides Clear all with confirmation.
- The old top-level Settings > Clear notifications row is removed; Clear all belongs in
  Notified logs.
- Home metadata adds a small '/ notified' marker to rows currently represented in the
  notification area.

APP-LAUNCH NOTIFICATION DEDUPLICATION

- There is at most one durable app-launch notification per package.
- Notifying a newer RFS launch row transfers the durable notified state from an older RFS
  launch row to the newer one.
- App-launch Android notification tags are package-derived, so the newer card updates the
  existing launcher card instead of creating duplicate RFS launcher cards.
- Unrelated app-launch notifications and ordinary notified log entries remain independent.
- Deleting any journal row now reconciles Android cards from durable journal state; deleting
  an old RFS launch row cannot accidentally cancel a newer RFS notification.

PRESERVED

- Notify/Unnotify remains reversible on the source entry.
- Still-notified rows are reconstructed after process death or force-stop followed by relaunch.
- MML's own notifications do not re-enter the journal.
- Grouped display rows have no synthetic Notify.
- Actionable Home rows keep the dark background and two-gray checkered border.
- Detail blank-space taps use the same contextual action as detail text.
- The stable version-1 .log.txt format is unchanged.
