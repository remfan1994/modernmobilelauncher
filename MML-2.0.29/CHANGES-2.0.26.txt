MML 2.0.26 changes
Prepared: 2026-11-11

NOTIFY / UNNOTIFY

- Notify is now a durable reversible state on one saved journal row.
- A row with notified_ms > 0 opens with an Unnotify button instead of Notify.
- Unnotify clears notified_ms and cancels only that row's UID-tagged Android notification.
- The log row itself is not deleted.
- Notify still keeps separate persistent notifications for separate saved entries.

DELETE

- After a journal delete succeeds, MML cancels Android notifications for every deleted row.
- This also handles rows whose in-memory notified flag is stale: cancellation uses the stable UID.

CLEAR NOTIFICATIONS

- Settings > Logs now includes Clear notifications.
- It confirms before clearing.
- Clear notifications resets notified_ms for all saved rows and removes only log-entry
  notifications; it does not delete journal rows or remove the persistent MML Settings control.

PROCESS RESTORE

- On a fresh Activity/process start, MML removes stale active log-entry notifications and pages
  through durable notified_ms > 0 rows in row order, reposting each persistent notification.
- This restores Notify state after process death or an explicit force-stop followed by relaunch.
- The reconciliation also removes orphan cards left by rows deleted by an older MML build.
- No boot receiver, polling loop, alarm, JobScheduler, WorkManager, wake lock, or foreground
  collector was added.

PRESERVED

- MML-owned ordinary notifications do not re-enter the journal.
- Pushed notifications remain ongoing and non-auto-canceling.
- Grouped display rows have no synthetic Notify.
- App-launch notification taps launch the recorded app without removing the card.
- Settings remains available through the persistent MML Settings notification and date long-press.
- Two-gray checkered action borders and the full detail-surface action path are unchanged.
